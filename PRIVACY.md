# MORPH privacy

MORPH converts and compresses files, including recognized text from bundled OCR, on your Android device. The app ships without Android Internet or network-state permissions and includes no MORPH analytics or telemetry. It has no MORPH account or upload service.

MORPH stores temporary conversion data, imported copies, conversion history, recipes, and Vault outputs locally. Android gives MORPH access to the files and folders you choose. A selected storage provider, browser, or recipient app may independently sync or transmit files when you open, save, or share them through that service.

The bundled OCR models process document contents locally. Google describes its SDK's general metrics practices in the [ML Kit Terms and Privacy notice](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms).
